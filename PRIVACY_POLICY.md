# 🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Protection Terms

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Welcome to **GeoRef Field** ("Application", "Service"), operated by **nilByte** ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use the **GeoRef** mobile application and backend services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location & Geographic Data
- **GPS Coordinates**: To provide core GIS mapping, field spatial collection, and line/polygon rendering, GeoRef accesses your device's high-accuracy GPS receiver.
- **Offline Spatial Datasets**: GeoPDFs, shapefiles, and map tile caches uploaded or generated by you are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your private backend server instance.

### B. Device & Technical Information
- Device Identifier (Client ID), Operating System version, and local app logs to facilitate idempotent sync and error diagnostics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- **Core Functionality**: To capture, display, and export georeferenced spatial features.
- **Offline Synchronization**: To synchronize local offline GIS records with your designated GeoRef backend instance.
- **Security & Integrity**: To validate topological geometries and prevent data corruption.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Ownership

- **Your Data Remains Yours**: nilByte does not sell, monetize, or harvest your spatial data or user records.
- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure**: You maintain complete control over the PostgreSQL/PostGIS server endpoints configured in your app settings.

---

## 4. Permissions Required

- **Location Services (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`)**: Required for real-time field positioning and spatial data collection.
- **Camera Access (`CAMERA`)**: Optional for capturing georeferenced photos attached to GIS points.
- **Storage Access (`READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Required to read local GeoPDF maps and export GeoJSON/KML files.
